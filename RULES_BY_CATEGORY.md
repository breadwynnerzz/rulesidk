**# Brick City RP Rules by Category**

Source of truth: this file. The website renders these rules through \`app.js\`.

**## Start Here — Brick City RP Server Rules**

\- Read **\*\*ALL RULES\*\*** before joining the city. These rules keep Brick City fair, fun, and enjoyable for everyone.

\- We **\*\*HIGHLY RECOMMEND\*\*** keeping clipping software on. Staff decisions are based mostly on proof and recorded interactions.

\- **\*\*NO CLIP = NO SIT.\*\*** If there is no proof, staff may not be able to help.

**## General Rules — Clipping System / Reports / Tickets**

1\. Use clipping software such as NVIDIA, AMD, Medal, or something similar. Reports without proof are **\*\*INVALID\*\***.

2\. A clip should usually have at least two minutes of footage and make it clear who was involved.

3\. Clips need the important audio and enough context to show what happened.

4\. Do not make reports just to get revenge. Reports must be sent within 6 hours of the incident.

5\. A rule break does not automatically end a scene. Finish the scene when you reasonably can, then report it afterward.

6\. Spamming reports or abusing the ticket system may get your reports ignored and may lead to punishment.

7\. Anti-cheat bans may be reviewed by management. Account history and prior detections may be considered during appeals.

8\. Verification teams reserve the right to deny verification requests that cannot be reasonably validated.

**## Main Rules**

1\. Treat people with respect. Everyone in Brick City RP must be 16 or older. Anyone under 16 will be permanently removed.

2\. Do not argue with staff rulings while a situation is still active.

3\. Do not go AFK on purpose to avoid a roleplay situation.

4\. Do not use macros, keybinds, or exploits to get an unfair advantage.

5\. Report bugs and exploits right away. Do not use them to benefit yourself.

6\. Do not start a robbery, kidnapping, shooting, or other major crime within 15 minutes before or after a restart. A scene that started before the 15-minute warning may continue only if someone involved has a clip showing it was already happening.

**## Initiation**

1\. Any robbery, kidnapping, or killing needs at least **\*\*2 minutes of meaningful roleplay\*\*** first.

2\. You need a real roleplay reason. A quick or forced interaction made only to justify robbing, kidnapping, or killing someone does not count.

3\. You cannot initiate through Twitter, phone calls, texts, or social media.

4\. Pointing a gun at someone gives them a reason to fight back. They still cannot shoot or kill you just because you aimed at them; they must wait until it is clear you are trying to fight or shoot.

**### Faction Initiation**

1\. Faction membership alone does not give you initiation against another player.

2\. Outside of an active gang war, players may not target someone solely because they belong to an opposing faction.

3\. Valid roleplay and the required interactions must exist before attacking another faction member. Factions must have at least two negative roleplay interactions before initiating a conflict, and those interactions must be clipped with video and audio.

**## RDM / VDM / NLR**

**### Random Deathmatch (RDM)**

**\*\*Definition:\*\*** Random Deathmatch occurs when a player injures or kills another player without sufficient roleplay or proper initiation.

1\. Players must follow the initiation requirements before using lethal force.

2\. Players must have a valid roleplay reason before injuring or killing another player.

**### Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)**

**\*\*Definition:\*\*** Vehicle Deathmatch occurs when a player intentionally uses a vehicle to injure or kill another player.

1\. Purposely ramming another player or using vehicles as weapons is prohibited.

2\. A player aiming a gun at you while standing in front of the vehicle and you decide to drive forward and hit them is considered VDM. Someone standing in the road is not considered VDM, but be mindful.

**### New Life Rule (NLR)**

**\*\*Definition:\*\*** When your character dies, that character may not remember the events directly surrounding their death.

1\. Players may not immediately return to the location of their death.

2\. Information surrounding your death cannot be used for revenge purposes.

3\. Returning solely to continue the same conflict is prohibited.

4\. After respawning at the hospital, players may not return to the scene, retrieve items or vehicles from it, or become involved in the same conflict for 15 minutes.

5\. Players revived by EMS must realistically roleplay their injuries but do not automatically lose all memory of the scene.

**## Fear RP / Fail RP**

**### Fear RP / Fail RP**

**\*\*Fear RP Definition:\*\*** You must value your character's life and react reasonably during dangerous situations.

**\*\*Fail RP Definition:\*\*** Performing unrealistic, impossible, or immersion-breaking actions.

1\. If you are clearly outnumbered or overpowered, act like your character wants to live.

2\. React realistically when weapons or serious danger are involved.

3\. Ignoring an obvious threat just to keep fighting can be Fail RP.

4\. If a player is attempting auto theft, the owner has the right to use lethal force after 2 verbal warnings.

5\. In a 1v1 situation, you may defend yourself by all means if your weapon is already withdrawn. If not, you must fear for your life.

6\. In a 1v2 situation, you must fear for your life unless your weapon is already withdrawn.

7\. Having your weapon out only applies to 1v1 and 1v2 situations, not anything more.

8\. If 3 or more people have you outnumbered, you **\*\*MUST\*\*** fear for your life. Ignoring this can be Fail RP and may mean you give up your pockets.

9\. If both parties have an equal amount to each other, it is free game. Die trying.

10\. If PD or EMS arrive after a shootout has ended, all remaining involved survivors must leave the scene immediately.

11\. Players **\*\*MUST\*\*** fear the police.

12\. Players are not allowed to rob, punch, kidnap, shoot, threaten, or kill players on a crutch or wheelchair unless they bring it upon themselves.

13\. Players on a crutch or wheelchair lose this protection if actively trapping or giving callouts over the radio.

14\. Handcuffed players may not operate any motor vehicle, bike, or radio. This is Power Gaming and FRP.

**### Injury and Downed Roleplay**

1\. Roleplay your injuries after shootings, fights, serious crashes, or medical situations.

2\. Downed players cannot give callouts, use radios or phones, move items, or keep influencing an active scene.

3\. Players may not troll, disrespect, or use emotes over an unconscious player.

**## Metagaming / Powergaming**

**### Metagaming**

**\*\*Definition:\*\*** Bringing information learned outside of roleplay into roleplay.

Examples listed on the site:

\- Discord communications

\- Streams

\- Screen shares

\- Voice calls

\- External chats

Information learned outside of roleplay may not be used to gain advantages in character.

Players may recognize a masked person through legitimate in-character familiarity, but may not use player IDs, Discord names, streams, or other out-of-character information to identify them. Character-to-character information use is governed by Character Separation.

**### Powergaming**

**\*\*Definition:\*\*** Performing actions that would be impossible, unrealistic, or not reasonably achievable.

Examples listed on the site:

\- Providing locations while incapacitated

\- Entering protected areas solely to avoid consequences

\- Selling illegal items inside protected zones

\- Operating vehicles while restrained

\- Unrealistic movement or actions

\- Selling drugs on mountains, tunnels, and similar locations

**## Robbing / Scamming / Logging / Exploits**

**### Character Separation**

1\. Each character/soul is a separate person.

2\. Do not move information, relationships, money, items, or assets from one character to another unless the server mechanic allows it. Moving assets to get ahead unfairly or bypass server rules is **\*\*STRICTLY PROHIBITED\*\***.

3\. Do not switch characters to escape an active scene, investigation, debt, punishment, or other roleplay consequence.


**### Robbing & Scamming**

**\*\*Robbing:\*\*** Taking another player's stuff through threats or force.

**\*\*Scamming:\*\*** Lying to another player during a deal or trade.

**\*\*Kill OR rob:\*\*** You can kill someone or rob them during an interaction, but not both. If you kill, do not rob them. If you rob them, do not kill them.

1\. Robberies must follow the initiation rules and include at least 2 minutes of meaningful roleplay.

2\. You need a real roleplay reason before starting a robbery.

3\. You cannot fight, rob, and kill someone in the same interaction without proper escalation.

4\. If someone is cooperating, you cannot just execute them.

5\. You cannot force someone to sell assets, withdraw money, or produce items they do not have on them.

6\. Robberies involving the same players should be reasonably spaced apart.

7\. Scamming server-owned assets, administrative items, or prohibited items is not allowed.

8\. Brick City is not liable for transactions that take place outside of the city.

9\. Players may only take money, items, or assets physically present during the robbery. You may not force someone to withdraw money, open personal storage, enter a house, or retrieve a vehicle from a garage. A vehicle already outside may be taken through proper roleplay.

10\. Players may not rob someone who just spawned, was just revived by EMS, or is actively performing a legal scripted job.


**### Kidnappings and Hostages**

1\. Kidnappings need a real roleplay reason. Random kidnappings, or kidnappings done just to bait police or start a shootout, are not allowed.

2\. A hostage can be held for up to 30 minutes.

3\. Fake hostages are prohibited.

4\. During a robbery involving police, tell officers how many people are involved and who is inside or outside. Do not hide or lie about outside shooters, extra people, or anyone else taking part.


**### Combat Logging**

1\. Combat logging can lead to punishment and possible inventory loss.

2\. If you crash or disconnect during an active scene, come back and continue it. If you cannot return, tell staff or someone involved within 10 minutes. If you do neither, it may count as combat logging.

3\. Combat logging on police at any time will result in **\*\*IMMEDIATE BAN\*\***.

4\. Wait at least 10 minutes after an active scene fully ends before logging out.

**### Combat and Scene Involvement**

1\. Do not jump into an active shootout, robbery, pursuit, or police scene unless you were already involved or had a clear roleplay connection before it started.

2\. Driving toward active gunfire or joining a fight after the shooting starts, without being involved already, is Fail RP.

3\. Shooting from the driver's seat of a vehicle is prohibited. Passengers may shoot from vehicles that are designed and configured to allow passenger shooting.

**### Exploiting**

**\*\*Definition:\*\*** Using game mechanics, bugs, or unintended features to get an advantage.

Examples listed on the site:

\- Duplication glitches

\- Clipping through objects

\- Abuse of emotes

\- Fake NPC interactions

\- Economy exploits

\- Modding / Mod Menus / Triggers

**## Greenzones / Third-Party Mods**

**### Greenzones**

Police stations, hospitals, vehicle garages, and player-owned businesses that are currently open are **\*\*GREENZONES\*\***.

**\*\*Definition:\*\*** Protected areas where criminal activity is not allowed.

Greenzone locations listed on the site:

\- Hospital

\- Vehicle Garages

\- City Hall

\- Vehicle Dealerships

\- Designated Businesses while open and actively operating

\- Administrative Event Areas

\- Casino except parking lot

\- Player-Owned Businesses while open and actively operating

\- Tattoo Shop

\- Hair Shop

\- Clothing Store

Closed businesses are regular city locations. Banks are not Greenzones. Moneywash and Cayo Perico are **\*\*RED ZONES\*\***.

If a scene starts outside a Greenzone and moves into one, you cannot use the Greenzone to escape the scene. Staff may review the situation based on what happened.

**### Red Zones**

Moneywash and Cayo Perico are high-risk **\*\*RED ZONES\*\***.

1\. In a Red Zone, you may start a robbery right away without the usual 2 minutes of roleplay.

2\. You still need to make a clear demand and give the other person a chance to respond. You cannot pull up and shoot someone right away.

3\. If the person cooperates, you cannot kill them. If they fight back, pull a weapon, or try to escape after your demand, you may defend yourself.

4\. Being in a Red Zone does not make anyone KOS. If you came there planning to kill someone, you still need a valid reason and some roleplay first.

5\. PD still follows its normal use-of-force rules. EMS waits until the scene is safe.

**\*\*Example:\*\*** If someone is farming at Cayo and you run up, pull a gun, and tell them to put their hands up for a robbery, that is allowed without the 2-minute buildup. They can still fight back if they believe they have a fair chance. You cannot shoot them before making the demand and giving them a chance to react.

**### Third-Party Modifications**

**\*\*Definition:\*\*** Any software or modification providing unfair advantages.

Examples listed on the site:

\- Cheats

\- Tracers

\- ESP

\- Aimbots

\- Exploit menus

\- No Props

The use of unauthorized modifications will result in **\*\*PERMANENT BAN\*\***.

**## Faction Association Rules**

1\. Official faction members need to represent their faction properly.

2\. A faction may have a maximum of **8 registered players total**. This is the total faction roster limit, not an eight-player scene limit.

3\. Faction members must wear a visible faction identifier during faction activity. The faction flag is the minimum.

4\. Players who leave, get removed from, or are DP’d from a faction must wait one week before joining another one.

5\. Civilians entering active faction territory accept the risks that come with being in a dangerous area.

6\. Civilians may sell drugs, purchase weapons, and rob other civilians through valid roleplay.

7\. Civilians may not rob, shoot, or kill gang or faction members unless they are directly retaliating to an action initiated against them.

8\. Gang and faction members may not rob civilians without valid, meaningful roleplay. Randomly targeting civilians for robbery is prohibited.

**### Faction Registration and Disbandment**

1\. A faction becomes official after its gang package is bought through Tebex, claimed through a ticket, and set up.

2\. Only registered factions may represent themselves as factions or use faction benefits.

3\. When a faction is disbanded, the disbandment and reason will be posted in the proper Discord channel.

4\. Once disbanded, members cannot represent or operate as that faction.

**### Faction Conflict**

1\. Faction conflicts must build through real roleplay.

2\. Factions cannot repeatedly bait, drone, disrespect, provoke, or attack another faction just to force a war.

3\. Being near a faction, wearing similar colors, or knowing a faction member does not automatically make a civilian affiliated with that faction.

4\. Faction leadership is responsible for repeated rule violations committed by its members. Multiple related violations may result in faction strikes or other faction consequences.

**### Active Faction Scenes**

1\. Factions cannot send another group to continue a scene after the original people are down, arrested, gone, or the scene ended. This is a wave attack.

2\. If you were not involved or contacted before the scene began, do not show up just because your faction is involved. If you were not called before it started, stay out.

3\. Factions cannot use civilians, unofficial members, alternate characters, or outside players to do faction activity for them or get around faction rules.

**### Alliances**

1\. A faction can have only one official alliance at a time. If you are already allied, you cannot make or join another alliance.

2\. Alliances cannot be used to get around faction rules or bring uninvolved players into active scenes.

**### Gang Wars**

1\. Gang wars must be created and accepted through the in-game gang tablet.

2\. Both factions must confirm the war through the tablet before it begins.

3\. Only legitimate registered members of the participating factions may participate.

**### Location Camping**

Players may not camp drug locations, job locations, garages, hospitals, businesses, or other high-traffic areas solely to rob, ambush, or repeatedly target players. Official faction territories are governed by the faction rules.

**## Government Job Rules**

**### Interaction With Police & EMS**

1\. You may not take weapons or equipment from police. Radios and cellphones are the exception.

2\. Kidnapping a government employee needs meaningful roleplay. Do not rob, kidnap, shoot, or kill EMS while they are doing medical work unless management approved the event.

3\. If EMS revives you, keep roleplaying your injuries. Do not avoid using a crutch or wheelchair.

4\. Pretending to be PD, EMS, or another government worker is not allowed.

5\. Do not interfere with active staff scenes, staff sits, or PD scenes. If you are not involved, leave and let the scene continue.

6\. Do not interfere with active police scenes using lethal weapons. If PD is chasing your friend, you cannot just shoot at them. Only the person being chased can shoot, and they cannot shoot first. Tasers do not count.

7\. Do not take, loot, hide, move, or mess with police bodies after a scene. Downed officers are not loot, leverage, or trophies.

8\. Do not bait cops.

**### Department Conduct and Integrity**

1\. Government employees cannot abuse their powers, access, or resources.

2\. Corruption storylines need management approval first. Staff will not automatically tell department leadership. Approval does not excuse unrelated rule-breaking.

3\. On-duty criminal activity is not allowed unless it is part of a management-approved corruption storyline.

4\. Do not use department information or resources to help friends or factions. This includes releasing or uncuffing friends or warning people about warrants, raids, or police activity unless it is part of an approved corruption storyline.

5\. You cannot be in PD and a faction at the same time, even on different characters or souls.

6\. Do not switch off duty to escape an active department scene, investigation, or consequence.

7\. Police cannot make up evidence, file false charges, change records, lie about a scene, or hide misconduct.

**### PD Searches and Warrants**

1\. PD may search a person or vehicle after a lawful arrest, when illegal items or weapons are visible, when GSR or other evidence links them to a scene, with consent, with an approved warrant, or when valid roleplay gives them a real reason to search.

2\. A downed or hospitalized player may be searched only after the scene is safe and valid legal grounds for the search exist.

3\. Search and raid warrants need PD High Command approval. Raiding a house, business, or faction property also needs proof gathered through valid roleplay. Emergency entry is allowed when someone is in immediate danger.

4\. A dispatch alert can start an investigation, but it is not enough by itself to search or arrest someone. PD needs more proof through valid roleplay.

5\. PD cannot camp or repeatedly watch illegal spots or faction territory without an active investigation and proof connected to that place or group.

**### Evidence, Confiscation, and Vehicle Seizure**

1\. After a lawful search, PD may take illegal drugs, dirty money, illegal or unlicensed weapons, ammo tied to the crime, government equipment, and crime-related tools or evidence.

2\. PD cannot take clean money or unrelated legal items.

3\. VIP weapons are subject to seizure like any other weapon unless the player has the VIP Weapon License on them at the time of search and seizure.

4\. PD may impound or take a vehicle if it was used in a crime, the driver was lawfully arrested and no owner is around, it has crime evidence inside, it is illegally parked or abandoned, or an approved warrant allows it. Vehicles cannot be taken just to punish someone.

5\. Put seized evidence somewhere safe and log it. Officers cannot keep, hide, destroy, plant, improperly move, or use seized evidence. Messing with evidence is serious department misconduct.

**### Charges, Sentencing, and Evidence**

1\. Officers must follow the penal code.

2\. Officers cannot knowingly file charges with no proof, make up charges, stack duplicate charges, or purposely raise fines or jail time.

3\. Officers cannot change charges or sentencing because of an OOC argument.

4\. Before sending someone to jail, PD must tell them the charges, fine, and sentence.

**### Detention and Downed Suspects**

1\. Detaining or cuffing someone needs a real roleplay reason. Explain why when you reasonably can.

2\. Do not abandon someone you have restrained. Let them go when there is no longer a reason to hold them.

3\. Do not use handcuffs to punish or silence someone.

4\. PD can keep a downed suspect in lawful custody while EMS treats them. EMS treatment comes before questions or processing.

5\. The scene must be safe and PD needs a valid reason to search someone who is downed. Once EMS clears them, police processing can continue.

**### Use of Force**

1\. PD may use deadly force when there is an immediate deadly threat, such as a suspect shooting or aiming at PD, using a vehicle as a weapon, drawing or reaching for a weapon, or threatening to use one.

2\. Simply having a weapon holstered or stored does not justify deadly force.

3\. PD may use a taser when someone runs from a lawful detention or arrest, physically resists, or reaches for a weapon.

**### Pursuits and PITs**

1\. PD must wait at least two minutes before attempting a PIT.

2\. Do not PIT when the conditions are too dangerous. There is no fixed speed limit, but the PIT must be reasonably safe for the situation.

3\. Do not ram unrealistically. PD must roleplay serious crashes and vehicle damage.

4\. No more than five ground units may participate in a pursuit.

5\. One air unit may assist in addition to the five ground units.

6\. Use spike strips and roadblocks only when they are reasonably safe and fit the situation.

7\. PD must slow down or end a pursuit if continuing is too dangerous. PD may continue if the suspect is an immediate serious threat to other people.

**### Hostage and Robbery Scenes**

1\. PD must honestly negotiate during hostage and robbery scenes.

2\. PD must honor agreed demands while the hostage is safe and everyone is following the agreement.

3\. PD may breach when negotiations fail or an immediate threat starts.

4\. PD cannot fake negotiations just to stall for more officers or resources.

**### Undercover Investigations**

1\. Undercover operations need PD High Command approval first. Major operations or storylines also need management approval.

2\. An undercover officer may do what is reasonably needed to keep their cover during an approved operation.

3\. Undercover officers cannot pressure, bait, or trick someone into a crime they were not already trying to commit.

**### Department Property**

1\. Only approved employees may drive government vehicles.

2\. Do not give or sell government equipment to civilians or unauthorized players, store it for another character, or keep it after leaving the department. Giving out government equipment is a **\*\*BANNABLE OFFENSE\*\***.

3\. Do not use government vehicles or equipment for personal reasons.

**### Off-Duty Conduct**

1\. Off-duty PD and EMS are civilians. They cannot use department vehicles, restricted equipment, databases, dispatch, uniforms, or department authority.

2\. Off-duty personnel may use their own legal civilian items and weapons under the normal server rules.

3\. You cannot go off duty to avoid an active department scene, investigation, or consequence.

**### PD Documentation and Bodycam**

1\. Log every arrest and all seized evidence. Not doing the required logs means automatic removal from PD.

2\. The officer involved should have their own recording. Another involved officer's recording may be accepted.

3\. The recording must show the full important interaction. Edited or intentionally incomplete recordings do not count.

**### EMS Scene Safety and Patient Care**

1\. EMS may enter once PD says the scene is safe or EMS reasonably believes it is safe. EMS must not enter an active shootout.

2\. EMS must treat everyone fairly. Do not refuse, delay, change, or prioritize treatment to punish someone or help friends or factions.

3\. When there are multiple patients, treat the most urgent ones first. Follow scene command when it is not safe to reach someone.

4\. EMS cannot refuse treatment because of an old conflict.

5\. EMS cannot revive or move someone just to send them back into a fight or give someone a tactical advantage.

6\. EMS may pause treatment if a patient is actively threatening them or the scene is unsafe. Once it is reasonably safe, EMS must provide fair medical care.

7\. EMS decides when a patient is cleared for police processing. PD may continue once EMS clears them.

8\. After a shootout, EMS may treat or revive people at the scene only after it has been declared reasonably safe.

**### EMS Custody, Privacy, and Prohibited Assistance**

1\. A patient in police custody stays in custody during treatment. PD may ride in or follow the ambulance. EMS cannot release or help them escape.

2\. EMS may share information with PD when it is needed for immediate scene safety or because the patient is in custody. Government employees cannot leak other private information learned on duty.

3\. EMS cannot take a patient's property or money, search for PD, carry or use illegal weapons on duty, or hold someone for PD without a real custody reason.

4\. EMS cannot pass information to friends or factions, hide or move bodies for friends, help someone escape custody, or give improper help to friends or factions.

**### Microphone Rules**

Players **\*\*MUST\*\*** have a functioning microphone free of excessive echo, static, or disruptive noise.

**## OOC / TOS / Other**

**### Out of Character (OOC)**

1\. Stay in character at **\*\*ALL TIMES\*\***.

2\. Do not use OOC chat for insults, harassment, or arguments.

3\. Repeatedly going OOC during roleplay can lead to punishment.

4\. Toxic behavior on any platform connected to Brick City RP can get you removed from the community.

5\. Character names must be realistic and follow server guidelines.

**### Terms of Service (TOS)**

By joining Brick City RP, you agree to follow all server rules and community standards.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, leaking personal information, impersonation of staff, hate speech, cheating, or severe toxicity may result in removal from the community. Any of these violations will result in a permanent ban.

**### Other**

1\. Female exclusive items are to only be used by female characters.

2\. If a female is robbed during a roleplay scene, only a female may take female exclusive items from another female. If a male takes them during a built-up scene, the items must be given to another female during the scene.

3\. Players may roleplay as any gender as long as it is done respectfully and realistically. Trolling, harassment, deception, or disruptive use of voice changers is prohibited.

4\. Terrorism roleplay is not allowed. **\*\*BANNABLE OFFENSE\*\***.

5\. Any form of real life threats will not be tolerated. **\*\*BANNABLE OFFENSE\*\***.

6\. Scenarios such as rape or obscene roleplay are prohibited. **\*\*BANNABLE OFFENSE\*\***.
